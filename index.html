How to try it

  1. Click Send stimulus. Watch the shapes wobble, then snap into the memory. The bar for that memory climbs past 90%.
  2. Turn the damage up to 30% or 40% and send again. Sometimes it still recognizes the memory. Sometimes it gets confused.
  3. Untick Drive on. The time crystal melts, the lock drops, and the brain can no longer hold a memory. Tick it again and it recovers.
  4. Press Random event, then Imprint the shape it settles into. You just taught it a fourth memory.

What's real physics here

  • RealThe core is a classical discrete time crystal. Driven at one rhythm, it locks to exactly half that rhythm, and it stays locked even with noise and uneven parts.
  • RealMemories are stable phase patterns in coupled oscillators. This is oscillatory associative memory, a known neural-network model.
  • RealThe crystal's half-rhythm pushes every clock toward one of two positions. Oscillator Ising machines compute this way.

What's from the book

  • IdeaClocks inside clocks, with ring rhythms set by prime numbers (2, 3, 5). This nods to the Phase Prime Metric.
  • IdeaEvents as shapes, and decisions as the network resonating into a new shape.
  • Not builtThe physical brain jelly and Hinductor. This is a software model of the ideas, not the hardware, and not a conscious machine.
