    What is real here

    The rings are a real simulation: 144 coupled oscillators. A drive pushes them at one rhythm and they answer at half that rhythm, which is how a classical time crystal behaves. A stored pattern of “up” and “down” clocks acts as memory. Turn the drive off and the slow ring slowly loses the goal; the agent notices and says so.

    The searches are real. They go to Wikipedia, and every sentence in the brief is copied from the article it cites, so you can check it. The critic confirms each sentence really appears in its source before the brief is accepted.

    There is no large language model inside yet. Version 0.1 writes the brief by choosing and citing real sentences rather than writing new ones. A language model can be plugged in later as the part that explains and rewrites, while NANOBRAIN keeps the goal and the working memory.
